Questions

What companies ask before working with us.

What does VelocityCraft do? +

VelocityCraft is a strategic marketing studio. We position growth-focused companies to be seen, trusted, and valued, combining marketing strategy, digital and AI visibility, and investor positioning.

Who do you work with? +

Growth companies that want to stand out and be trusted, from startups sharpening their market presence to public companies on OTC and Nasdaq markets strengthening how the market perceives them.

How is this different from a traditional agency or content shop? +

We lead with strategy and positioning, then build the visibility to match. Our work is grounded in credibility, including rare fluency in how investors and capital markets read a company.

Can you help with investor positioning and uplisting? +

Yes. Investor positioning is a core discipline. We help OTC and Nasdaq issuers and capital-stage ventures translate performance into investor-ready narratives and improve market perception ahead of uplisting or capital access. VelocityCraft is not a registered investment advisor and does not provide investment, legal, or tax advice.

How do engagements start? +

With a conversation. We learn your goals and market, assess your positioning and visibility, and propose a focused roadmap. Most engagements begin within one to two weeks.
